# Cook & Bake Academy — Market Brief

**Positioning:** Singapore's only cooking/baking school built around structured 2–8 week skill progressions, in a market that is otherwise almost entirely single 2–4 hour sessions.

## Personas

1. **Skill-Ladder Hobbyist** (28–45, working professional) — wants technique, not just a recipe; pays for a multi-week arc that ends in a real skill.
2. **Experience Gifter** (25–38) — books one low-commitment session as a gift, date, or self-treat.
3. **Team-Bonding Buyer** (HR/office manager, 10–70 pax) — books on behalf of a company; a separate B2B channel at S$80–150/pax for a 2–3hr session.

## Launch first (5 courses)

1. **CUL-210** Knife Skills & Kitchen Essentials — S$160 · 1 wk · Bukit Timah — entry/trial product
2. **BAK-108** Cookie & Biscuit Baking — S$180 · 1 wk · Orchard Road — gift-ready
3. **BAK-101** Artisan Sourdough Bread Baking — S$680 · 4 wks · Orchard Road — signature structured course
4. **CUL-202** Thai Street Food Cooking — S$540 · 3 wks · Bukit Timah — broad-appeal
5. **CUL-209** Grilling & BBQ Mastery — S$460 · 2 wks · Bukit Timah — social/team format

## Pricing notes

Our per-session-equivalent price (fee ÷ session count) runs S$160–245, at the upper edge of a market clustered S$90–250 across named competitors. The two entry-price courses (S$160–180, single session) are the cheapest way in; the rest are priced as structured multi-week learning, not drop-in classes.

## What the website must do to convert

1. **Sell the format, not just the class.** Multi-week commitment has no real market precedent — each course page needs a week-by-week breakdown of what's learned, not just a single blurb.
2. **Surface the low-commitment entry path first.** Route first-time visitors toward the S$160–180 single-session courses before the multi-week ones.
3. **Give the corporate buyer its own path.** Team-bonding bookings are a distinct S$80–150/pax B2B channel and need a separate enquiry flow from consumer registration.
