# Cook & Bake — brand notes for the website

## Voice
Warm, practical, confident. Talk like a chef who likes teaching.
Short sentences. No hype words ("amazing", "world-class").

## Colours
- Crust (primary):      #B5652D
- Crust dark (hover):   #8F4B1F
- Ink (text):           #2B211B
- Cream (background):   #FBF7F1
- Basil (focus, OK):    #3F6B4A

## Type
- Headings: Georgia (serif), bold.
- Body: the system sans-serif stack.

## Photos
Real food, natural light, hands at work. Never stock people smiling at
the camera. The site uses Unsplash images by id — see hero-images.md.
